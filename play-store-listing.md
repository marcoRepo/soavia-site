Do you get car sick or feel nauseous on winding roads? Are you anxious about long tunnels or high bridges? SafeRoute is the navigator designed for people who want to travel comfortably, not just quickly.

Unlike regular navigation apps, SafeRoute doesn't just pick the fastest route. It analyzes every road and shows you in advance how many curves, tunnels and bridges you'll encounter, how long they are and how long they'll last. So you can decide before you leave which route suits you best.

FIVE COMFORT PROFILES

• Nausea free — Avoids curves and hairpin turns. Designed for those who suffer from car sickness, motion sickness or kinetosis. Great for children who get nauseous in the car.

• Tunnel free — Routes that avoid long tunnels. If you're afraid of tunnels, suffer from claustrophobia or simply prefer driving in the open air, this profile is for you.

• Bridge free — Avoids major bridges and viaducts. For those who are afraid of heights, high bridges or exposed viaducts.

• Balanced — The best compromise between comfort and travel time. Avoids extremes without adding too much to the journey.

• City Relax — For city driving: less standstill traffic, fewer turns, no restricted zones. Relaxed routes even in urban chaos.

HOW IT WORKS

Enter your starting point and destination. SafeRoute calculates multiple alternative routes and ranks them by comfort, not speed. For each route you see:

— A comfort score from 0 to 100
— The number and length of tunnels, bridges and curve zones
— A visual stress bar showing where the demanding sections are
— A narrative timeline telling you what to expect along the way
— Guided navigation with voice alerts before tunnels and bridges

You can adjust filters: choose the minimum tunnel or bridge length you consider challenging, and routes adapt in real time.

— People planning long trips who want to know what to expect before leaving
